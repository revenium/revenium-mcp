# Ask Revenium for top customers by cost (last 30 days)

Get monthly customer cost breakdown from Revenium.

Use the business_analytics_management tool with these parameters:
- action: "get_customer_costs"
- period: "THIRTY_DAYS"
- group: "TOTAL"

## Use Case
Customer billing analysis, revenue optimization, account management planning.

## Deliverables
Review the output of the tool call and provide the user a comprehensive overview of all information provided.
